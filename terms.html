02.

Platform License Grant & SaaS Access

Subject to the terms and timely payment of subscription fees, Exxomatic grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable, worldwide commercial license during the subscription term to:

  • Access and utilize the NavEdge 2.0 Fleet Operating System for authorized corporate fleet monitoring, dispatching, and diagnostic reporting.
  • Integrate internal enterprise resource planning (ERP), transport management systems (TMS), and logistics applications via authorized ExxoCloud REST & WebSocket APIs.
  • Download and utilize official Exxomatic driver dispatch and supervisor mobile applications on authorized corporate mobile devices.

Customer shall not reverse-engineer, decompile, disassemble, rent, sublicense, or distribute the platform software or proprietary machine learning models without express prior written consent from Exxomatic.

03.

ExxoEdge™ Hardware Warranty & Supply

Exxomatic designs, manufactures, and supplies industrial-grade automotive IoT edge devices, including the ExxoEdge™ 4G/5G AIS-140 vehicle tracking unit, CAN bus non-intrusive contact readers, and EV battery telemetry modules:

  • Hardware Warranty: Exxomatic warrants that ExxoEdge™ devices shall be free from manufacturing and component defects under normal automotive operating conditions for a period of twenty-four (24) months from the date of initial dispatch.
  • Exclusions: Warranty coverage explicitly excludes physical tampering, incorrect vehicle electrical wiring, reverse polarity damage, submersion beyond rated IP67 specifications, or lightning surge incidents.
  • RMA Replacement: Defective devices reported within the warranty window shall be repaired or replaced on a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) priority basis within seven (7) business days from receipt at our Pune testing laboratory.
  • M2M SIM Connectivity: Where Exxomatic supplies pre-configured embedded eSIMs or roaming M2M telecom profiles, data usage is restricted exclusively to vehicle telematics payloads. Unauthorized extraction or secondary use of SIMs constitutes immediate breach and termination.
04.

Firmware & Firmware Over-The-Air (FOTA)

ExxoEdge™ devices execute proprietary bare-metal real-time firmware designed to decode CAN-J1939 and OBD-II signals.

Cryptographically Signed FOTA: Customer grants Exxomatic authorization to deploy automated, cryptographically signed security patches, protocol updates, and AIS-140 compliance firmware over-the-air. All updates undergo pre-flight vehicle safety verification and automated rollback protection to ensure zero ECU disruption.
05.

AIS-140 Regulatory Mandate & Verification

For vehicles subject to statutory tracking under Indian Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) standards:

  • Customer Obligation: The Customer is strictly responsible for providing accurate Vehicle Identification Numbers (VIN), chassis numbers, and vehicle registration credentials for statutory VAHAN backend linking.
  • Emergency Button Wiring: In public transport passenger buses, Customer must ensure emergency panic switches and hooters remain physically wired and operational in accordance with AIS-140 test procedures.
  • Continuous Certification: Exxomatic maintains active Type Approval certificates from accredited testing agencies (ARAI / ICAT). We provide renewed compliance certificates upon statutory revisions.

Acceptable Use & API Rate Limits

Customers shall not utilize Exxomatic platforms or APIs to:

Term, Suspension & Order Exit

This Agreement commences upon the effective date of an Order and continues through the agreed term:
