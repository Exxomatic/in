Purpose-Built Solutions for Every Operating Environment

Every industry has distinct telematics challenges. Discover how Exxomatic combines custom CAN bus profiles, edge sensors, and dedicated cloud modules to deliver measurable ROI.

Commercial Logistics & Freight

Optimize long-haul interstate trucking routes, eliminate unauthorized deviations, and safeguard expensive cargo.

  • Corridor geofencing & ETA prediction
  • Automated electronic Proof of Delivery (ePoD)
  • Toll plaza & fastag trip reconciliation

Public Transit & Electric Buses

Deploy real-time telematics across municipal bus networks and zero-emission electric buses (e.g., Olectra & Tata Starbus).

  • Headway adherence & timetable tracking
  • Passenger Information System (PIS) integration
  • Depot fast-charging queue automation

Cold Chain & Pharma Transport

Maintain continuous compliance for temperature-sensitive drugs, biologics, dairy, and perishable food logistics.

  • Wireless BLE temperature probes (±0.2°C)
  • Door-opening sensor event triggers
  • Instant SMS/WhatsApp temperature excursion alerts

Heavy Machinery & Mining

Gain complete visibility into off-highway excavators, tippers, generators, and heavy quarry earthmovers.

  • Precise engine working vs idle hours
  • Hydraulic pressure & payload telemetry
  • Remote geo-lock & night engine mobilization

Employee Transport (ETS)

Automate corporate cab routing, shift schedules, female passenger safety protocols, and billing audits.

  • Automated route clustering & roster assignment
  • Panic SOS button with instant security center alert
  • Automated vendor trip billing & toll reconciliation

Vehicle Quota & Rental Fleets

Enforce contractual usage quotas, prevent odometer tampering, and streamline maintenance scheduling.

  • Digital CAN odometer synchronization
  • Automated monthly mileage quota deduction
  • Remote immobilizer trigger on overdue payment
