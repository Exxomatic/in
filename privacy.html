03.

CAN Bus & Telematics Telemetry Handling

Exxomatic systems interface directly with internal electronic control units (ECUs) across commercial diesel, hybrid, and electric vehicles via high-speed CAN networks. We implement strict data segregation protocols:

  • Read-Only Passive Ingestion: By default, ExxoEdge™ firmware operates in non-intrusive listen mode to guarantee vehicle engine management safety and warranty compliance.
  • Edge Filtering & De-identification: High-frequency 50Hz raw CAN signals are parsed and converted to standardized SI units at the edge. Raw bus message arbitration IDs that do not correlate to diagnostic requirements are discarded prior to uplink transmission.
  • Driver Identifier Decoupling: Telematics streams are indexed against unique Vehicle Identification Numbers (VIN) or Device IDs. Driver identity mappings are maintained in separate, role-restricted customer relational databases with zero cross-tenant contamination.

Legal Grounds for Processing

Under Section 4 and Section 7 of the Indian DPDP Act, 2023, and Article 6 of the GDPR, we process data under the following legitimate grounds:

  • Contractual Performance: Ingestion and computational analysis necessary to deliver the NavEdge 2.0 fleet platform, automated dispatching, driver scorecards, and maintenance predictions agreed in Master Services Agreements.
  • Statutory Mandates: Processing required under Indian Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH) standards (AIS-140) for public passenger and hazardous goods vehicles.
  • Legitimate Commercial Interests: Monitoring API traffic to prevent distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, detecting unauthorized SIM card extraction from ExxoEdge units, and optimizing telemetry transmission bandwidth.
  • Explicit Consent: Where enterprise fleet operators configure optional employee biometric or RFID driver authorization modules, explicit employee consent records are maintained by the respective enterprise subscriber.
05.

AIS-140 & Statutory Regulatory Sharing

ExxoEdge™ hardware units certified under Automotive Industry Standard AIS-140 contain dedicated dual-IP firmware pipelines designed for compliance with state and national tracking mandates:

Government Server Uplink: Where an enterprise vehicle is registered under state transport authority mandates (e.g. school buses, public transport, or petroleum tankers), our devices transmit real-time location and panic alert packets directly to authorized State Command & Control Centres (VLTD / VAHAN servers) as mandated by Indian Law.

Exxomatic does not sell, lease, or monetize telematics data or customer contacts to third-party marketing brokers, insurers, or data brokers under any circumstances.

06.

Customer Data Ownership Rights

The Customer is the sole owner of all raw telematics, route histories, and machine logs generated by their assets. Exxomatic claims no proprietary or intellectual property rights over customer fleet operational data.

Our customers possess uninterrupted rights to:

  • Full Export: Retrieve all raw CAN bus logs, GPS coordinates, and battery health reports via our RESTful APIs or scheduled Parquet/CSV s3 bulk dumps.
  • Purge upon Termination: Request complete deletion of tenant database partitions within thirty (30) calendar days of contract completion.
  • Aggregated Benchmarking: Exxomatic may utilize completely anonymized, aggregated statistical indices (e.g. average battery degradation curves across ambient temperatures) strictly for system optimization, ensuring no corporate entity or driver can ever be identified.
07.

Storage & Retention Schedules

Data is housed in Tier-IV enterprise cloud data centres physically located within the sovereign borders of India (Mumbai and Hyderabad regions) with zero unauthorized extraterritorial routing:

  • High-Frequency Telemetry: Raw second-by-second CAN bus streams are retained in hot query storage for 90 days, after which they automatically transition to cold compressed object storage for up to 365 days.
  • SOS & Emergency Incident Packets: Records generated during an emergency panic switch trigger or high-g collision event are preserved for a minimum of 3 years to assist statutory accident investigations.
  • Account Records: Corporate invoicing, tax records, and admin logs are retained for seven (7) years to comply with the Indian Companies Act and Goods and Services Tax (GST) audit mandates.
08.

Security & Cryptographic Standards

Security is embedded across every layer of the Exxomatic hardware and cloud architecture:

End-to-End Cryptography: All edge transmissions between ExxoEdge™ devices and ExxoCloud ingestors enforce mTLS 1.3 encryption with hardware-backed private keys stored inside dedicated Secure Elements (SE). Cloud databases enforce AES-256 encryption at rest.

Our infrastructure undergoes regular independent vulnerability assessment and penetration testing (VAPT) and is maintained under ISO 27001 information security practices.

Cookies & Portal Analytics
